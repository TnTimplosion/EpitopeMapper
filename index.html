Display

SASA by Shrake & Rupley (1973) with Bondi radii and a 1.4 Å probe; relative SASA against the reference areas of Tien et al. (2013). Hydrophobicity scales and their directions are listed in the README. Membrane geometry is read from the DUM dummy atoms written by PPM 3.0, whose coordinates place the bilayer normal along z. The electrostatic potential is Coulombic, with a distance-dependent dielectric (ε = 4r), Debye screening at 150 mM and formal group charges — useful for telling an acidic patch from a basic one, but not a Poisson-Boltzmann calculation.
